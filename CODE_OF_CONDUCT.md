# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can learn, disagree and improve things without fear.

## Our standard

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, not the person.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience; explain instead of belittling.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open, unless it is a security report (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; ask why, and keep it respectful.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language or images, spam, and
publishing someone else's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security tab) or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled privately and fairly.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from widely used community covenants.
